# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, technically honest, and considerate of differing backgrounds and experience. Focus criticism on work, avoid harassment and personal attacks, protect private operational data, and do not pressure anyone to disclose identity or access.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of credentials or private infrastructure, and sustained disruption are unacceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact on the repository profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.

This compact policy is inspired by common open-source community standards and applies to repository interactions and project-representing spaces.
